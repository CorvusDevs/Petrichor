Petrichor Support

Petrichor layers calm sounds for sleep, focus, and quiet moments on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

No sound?

Check your device volume and output route. If you use Bluetooth or AirPlay, reconnect the output and press Play again. Petrichor can mix with other audio when that option is enabled in Settings.

How do I stop playback?

Use the Pause control in Petrichor, the Lock Screen or Control Center controls, or clear the current mix. A sleep timer can fade playback out automatically.

How do I save a mix?

Set the levels you like, then choose Save this mix. Saved mixes, favorites, and settings stay on your device.
