Privacy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Our approach

Petrichor is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics SDKs, or a Petrichor-operated server. We do not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your sound levels, favorites, saved mixes, timer preferences, language choice, and app settings are stored on your device so the app can restore your soundscape.

Optional Health data

Only if you turn on “Log Mindful Minutes” and grant Health permission, Petrichor writes a completed sleep-timer session to Apple Health as a Mindful Minutes entry. Petrichor does not read Health data and does not send it to us.

Feedback

Choosing Feedback opens your email app with a prefilled message. Your email provider handles the message you choose to send.
